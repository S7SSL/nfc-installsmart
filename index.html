FAQ

Questions

Which phones work with it?

Any iPhone from the iPhone XS onwards and almost every Android phone with NFC switched on. There is nothing to install. On iPhone the screen needs to be unlocked and awake.

How long does delivery take?

We programme and test your card then send it by Royal Mail Tracked 48, which aims to deliver within two to three working days of posting. You get a tracking number by email.

Do you deliver outside the UK?

Not at the moment. We only ship to UK addresses.

Can I put my own logo on it?

Yes. Cards are sent blank in matt black so you can add your own logo sticker or decal. A sticker on the front will not stop the card working.

What if my link changes?

Email us and we will talk you through the options. If you are likely to change pages often, tell us when you order.

Can I cancel or get a refund?

All sales are final. Every card is coded to the link you give us, so once your order has been processed and the card has been coded we cannot cancel, refund or exchange it. Please check your link or @handle carefully before you pay. See our terms of sale.

What if the card does not work?

Every card is tested before it ships. If yours arrives faulty, email us within 30 days and we will replace it free of charge. This does not affect your legal rights.

Can I order cards for my team or several branches?

Yes. Up to 10 cards can go in one order if they all open the same page. For larger or mixed orders email sat@installsmart.ai.
